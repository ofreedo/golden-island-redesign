Website Redesign Proposal
The opportunity

Your current site captures the tranquil island feel guests love, but the phone number is hard to read against the ocean background, the layout feels dated, and there's no way to see the space online before walking in. A refresh keeps the vibe your guests already know — just easier to act on.

Old site vs. new site

Area Current site New site
Phone number Baked into the header photo — low contrast against the sky, can't be tapped to call Live, tappable number in a warm accent color on every page — sticky in the header
Pricing Same price list repeated on two separate pages One clear, organized pricing section with a "Most popular" package highlighted
Facilities Unlabeled photo grid — guests can't tell what they're looking at Captioned gallery (treatment room, reception, lounge) that builds trust before they arrive
Visual design Color palette feels scattered across pages One consistent ocean-and-sand palette, type, and layout system site-wide
Mobile experience No mobile menu — navigation disappears entirely on phones Dedicated mobile menu, tested and working on small screens
Location & hours Address and map feel like an afterthought Embedded map alongside hours and a one-tap call button

Why this matters for business

More calls, less friction
Since online booking isn't available, the phone number is the entire path to a booking. Making it legible and tappable on every page directly converts more visitors into calls.
A more premium first impression
One consistent palette, typography, and layout across every page signals a polished, established business — especially to first-time visitors still comparing options nearby.
Builds trust before they call
Captioned photos of the actual treatment rooms and reception area help guests feel confident booking before they've ever walked in the door.
Works for "massage near me" searches
Most local searches happen on a phone. The redesign includes a working mobile menu and a layout built and tested for smaller screens.
